# Changes to the Idea – August 5, 2026

## Overview
This document outlines the identified inconsistencies, logic errors, and architectural updates made to refine the core product concept and streamline the MVP stage.

---

## Key Updates

### 1. Authentication-Only Voting System
* **Issue:** Risk of bot activity, spamming, and duplicate voting manipulation.
* **Solution:** Voting is now strictly restricted to **authenticated users**. This ensures data integrity for the current build and sets up a scalable foundation for advanced user analytics in future stages.

### 2. Bracket-Style Tournament Structure (1v1 Competition)
* **Issue:** A single global leaderboard lacked engagement and long-term excitement for participants.
* **Solution:** Adopted a **sports tournament format**:
  1. **Stage 1 (Qualification):** All participants take a preliminary short quiz. Only the **top 16 scores** qualify for the next stage.
  2. **Stage 2 (Knockout Stage):** Qualified participants enter a **1v1 elimination bracket**, making the competition significantly more dynamic, engaging, and fun to follow.

### 3. Focused Domain Strategy for MVP
* **Issue:** Covering multiple fields simultaneously adds unnecessary complexity during initial launch.
* **Solution:** Scoped the **MVP strictly to Software Engineering**. Future seasons will cycle through different industries and domains to keep the platform fresh and dynamic.
